About

Brooke Chauntel

Brooke Chauntel makes art out of contradiction. Ink pieces where the hand has to commit. Mixed-media paintings where bodies turn into weather. Photographs that find a single moment already holding everything. She grew up paying attention to texture, pattern, and mood, and she makes things that try to pay it back.

She's a mother, a designer, and a lived-in student at Everett Community College, where she's earning a degree in Graphic & Web Design with a 4.0 GPA and President's Club three semesters running. Seven years of independent design practice lives under the art, the discipline that keeps the work from drifting.

She likes grit, contrast, tenderness, weathered things, black-and-white structure that suddenly floods with color. She doesn't like showroom beauty. She rock-hounds, forages, takes pictures, and pays attention. The work comes out of all of that.

Design is not decoration. It is architecture for meaning.
