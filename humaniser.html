How PASS works
Step 1

Forensic Analysis

Each sentence is examined for over-standardized markers — formulaic openings, clause symmetry, sterile register, uniform punctuation.

Step 2

Humanised Rewrite

A full rewrite targets surface structure and cadence. Technical claims, measurements, and citations are locked unchanged.

Step 3

Verification Table

Each detected trace is tracked as RESOLVED, REDUCED, or PARTIALLY RETAINED — with honest assessment of residual patterns.

Optional

Pass 2

A second pass targets residual patterns in the initial rewrite, continued only where meaningful improvement remains justified.
